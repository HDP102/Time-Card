Main thing is the web category review, which has changed shape since I met with Zachary from CSIRT last month.  We'd scoped it as custom category cleanup, but working through it the actual gap is that there's no governance model for URL categories at all, standard or custom, and no named owner for what gets blocked or why.

What I've been putting together is a framework across three dimensions. Business ownership per category, so HR owns adult, drugs and gambling, Compliance owns financial, and so on, and the decision sits with them rather than with us. Handling policy per category, hard block, browser isolation, coaching page or allow, instead of the binary we have today. And access tiers by user group, Open, Standard and High Access, rather than one policy across the board. Zachary has a legacy Blue Coat spreadsheet with the old category owner assignments, which gives us a starting baseline.

For sequencing I'd do a first pass with security, CSIRT and us, before bringing in HR, Compliance, OGC and End User Collaboration, so we're not taking a half formed model to them.

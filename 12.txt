Hardik, based on a research I just finished, you can enable the log monitoring at the management station level and it should map the usage for your child devices accordingly, you don't need to enable it on the 2400 devices one at a time.
 
About the RBAC, this is a feature that's been requested at a high priority and it's currently being worked on, but this was just requested 8 days ago. ETA for this is unknown.
 
How keys are used: FireMon retrieves the storage account access key dynamically via the Service Principal using the Azure Resource Manager listKeys API, you do not configure or store a static storage account key in FireMon. However, because listKeys returns a Shared Key for blob authentication, Allow storage account key access must remain enabled on the Storage Account.

Hey all, thanks for the call this morning. Here's how I'm planning to approach the escalation work, plus a few things I need settled before I build.

Plan
1. Escalation notifications: one script that sends the reminder stages and escalates when there's no response. For the test, each stage runs one day apart (60-day notice = day 1, 45 = day 2, and so on), so the full loop finishes in about 4 days with evidence captured at every step.
2. AD validation: before every notification, the script checks that the IT SME and IT SME backup accounts are active in AD, using a read-only lookup with the ticketAPI service account. If an account is disabled, it escalates to the backup/lead right away instead of waiting out the full cycle.
3. Disablement: only the IT SME and IT SME backup accounts, never the IT Lead or other leaders. The script won't disable accounts itself. At the final stage it hands the list to Gargi's MEA process, which disables the account and notifies the manager, and the direct leader re-enables it. I'll update the email wording to match.
4. Job failure alerts: a separate script that emails/Teams the team if the recertification jobs fail, so we know within the hour after a Tufin upgrade.

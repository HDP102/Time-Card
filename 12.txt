This supports CyberArk PAM discovery scanning from v50secwin050, v50secwin051 and v51secwin050 to all servers in AFDD.lan and AFDD.tst, crossing from Corporate LAN into the QA and Production DMZ zones. Discovery scanning requires reach across the estate and the named service ports for SMB, RDP, SSH, LDAP, Kerberos and SQL are consistent with CyberArk discovery, so the approach is sound. Two items need resolving before the rules are built. The port set includes the range 1024 to 65535, which is effectively all high ports and is not required by discovery, as CyberArk connects to known service ports rather than arbitrary ephemeral ports, so please remove that range or provide the vendor documentation requiring it. The destination is described as all servers in two domains with no enumeration, and the rule crosses inbound into the Production DMZ, so please confirm the specific DMZ segments in scope and that an estate wide destination is intended for this scanner rather than a defined server set. One record note, Internet Facing Application is set to Yes although this describes internal scanning traffic.
